# 020. A short FAQ

**Status:** removed (Ana, 2026-11-21): it repeated what "Ask my CV" already answers · **Roadmap:** phase 21

## Problem

janneparri.com closes with a short FAQ. A recruiter here asks the same few
things first: where and when she can work, her English, and the career
break. Today the answers are spread across the CV and the search.

## Scope

- **Four questions**, closed by default, before the closing card:
  1. Where, how and when do you want to work?
  2. What is your English level?
  3. Why is there a break since 2025?
  4. Can I see your code?
- **The CV's own words.** Answers 1–3 reuse the CV's sentences through
  their translation keys, so the FAQ can never say something the CV does
  not. Answer 4 points to the three projects and the GitHub profile.
- **Native `<details>`**, no script: keyboard, focus and screen readers work
  as they do anywhere else. A "+" turns into "×" when a question is open.
- **The editorial index** becomes six sections: FAQ is 05, and Let's talk
  moves to 06.
- **Not searchable, not printed**, so search results and the PDF do not
  change.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Four questions, all closed on arrival. Answers 1–3 equal the CV's text.
2. Enter opens and closes a question.
3. English questions and answers. The index lists six sections in both
   languages. axe passes, coverage stays at 100%, and Lighthouse stays at
   100.
